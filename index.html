About me

I am a term instructor at IU (Cybersecurity and Applied Cryptography) and a MA student in Political Philosophy at NKUA. It would have been the perfect combo for a career full of compassion and investigation of power dynamics, if it wasn't for my lack of academic background. In Cryptography, things look better, I have worked on topics around decentralization and privacy.

I have been a postdoctoral researcher at IRIF and a postdoctoral researcher with a joint appointment at NYU and GMU. I earned my PhD at GMU where I worked with Prof. Foteini Baldimtsi and I was a Protocol Labs fellowship award recipient. I hold a BS degree in Applied Informatics from UOM and a BS degree in Mathematics from AUTH. In Philosophy, I try.

Contact me if you are also interested in exploring what the limits of Cryptography are and where political begins, I am also willing to discuss whether everything was always political.
